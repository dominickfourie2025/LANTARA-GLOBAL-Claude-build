Who We Are

About Lantara Global

Lantara Global is an international education recruitment consultancy connecting Chinese schools with qualified native English-speaking teachers from trusted partner countries.

Our approach is built on ethical recruitment, careful vetting, and honest communication — not volume hiring. Every school and every teacher we work with receives our full attention, from first consultation through years of ongoing support on both sides.
