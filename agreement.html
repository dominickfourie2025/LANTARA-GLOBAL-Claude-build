Agreement Signed

 

1. Parties & Scope

This Recruitment Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into between Lantara Global ("Lantara") and the school or educational organisation registering as an employer on the Lantara Global platform ("Employer"). This Agreement governs the recruitment services Lantara provides to help the Employer identify, evaluate and hire qualified native English-speaking teaching candidates.

2. Recruitment Services

Lantara will source, screen and recommend candidates to the Employer based on the Employer's stated vacancy requirements. Recommendations may include a review of candidate qualifications, teaching experience, and other information voluntarily provided by candidates. Lantara does not guarantee the availability of any specific candidate or timeframe for placement.

3. Employer Responsibilities

The Employer agrees to provide accurate and current information about its organisation and vacancy requirements, to communicate professionally with candidates introduced through the platform, and to conduct its own final due diligence (including verification of qualifications, references and work eligibility) before extending any offer of employment.

4. Confidentiality

Candidate information shared with the Employer through the platform is provided solely for the purpose of evaluating that candidate for a specific vacancy and must not be shared with third parties or used for any other purpose without the candidate's consent.

5. Fees

Any recruitment fees applicable to the Employer will be communicated separately and are not established by the act of signing this Agreement.

6. Term & Termination

This Agreement remains in effect while the Employer maintains an active account on the Lantara Global platform. Either party may terminate this arrangement at any time by written notice.

7. Acceptance

By signing below, the Employer confirms that an authorised representative of the organisation has read, understood and agreed to the terms of this Agreement on behalf of the organisation named at registration.
