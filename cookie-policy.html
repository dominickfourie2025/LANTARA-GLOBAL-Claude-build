Cookie Policy

English / 简体中文

1. Purpose and Scope

This Cookie Policy explains how Lantara Global uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit or use the Lantara Global website.

It explains what cookies are, the categories of technologies we may use, why they are used, when consent is required, and how users can manage their preferences.

This policy should be read together with the Lantara Global Website Privacy Policy and Website Terms of Use.

Because the technologies used on a website may change over time, the actual cookies deployed on the website will be reviewed and updated as services and technical providers are added or removed.

目的与适用范围

本《Cookie 政策》说明您访问或使用 Lantara Global 网站时，Lantara Global 如何使用 Cookie 及类似技术。

本政策说明 Cookie 的含义、我们可能使用的技术类别、使用目的、何时需要获得用户同意，以及用户如何管理其偏好。

本政策应与 Lantara Global《网站隐私政策》及《网站使用条款》一并阅读。

由于网站所使用的技术可能随时间变化，随着服务及技术供应商的增加或删除，网站实际部署的 Cookie 将定期进行审查和更新。

2. What Are Cookies?

A cookie is a small text file or similar identifier that a website may store on a user's device or access from that device. Cookies can help websites remember information, maintain sessions, support security, remember preferences and understand how a website is used.

Similar technologies may include local storage, pixels, tags, scripts, SDKs or other technologies that store information on or access information from a user's device.

This policy uses the term “cookies” broadly to include these similar technologies where appropriate.

什么是 Cookie？

Cookie 是网站可能存储在用户设备上或从用户设备访问的小型文本文件或类似标识符。Cookie 可以帮助网站记住信息、维持会话、支持安全功能、记住用户偏好以及了解网站的使用情况。

类似技术可能包括本地存储、像素、标签、脚本、SDK 或其他在用户设备上存储信息或访问设备信息的技术。

在适当情况下，本政策所称“Cookie”也包括这些类似技术。

3. Our Approach to Cookies

Lantara Global follows a privacy-conscious approach to website technology.

We aim to use only technologies that are reasonably necessary for the website's operation, security and requested functionality, together with optional technologies that provide a legitimate business or user benefit.

Where applicable law requires consent for a non-essential cookie or similar technology, we will seek consent before activating that technology. Analytics and advertising/tracking technologies should not be treated as strictly necessary merely because they are useful to Lantara Global.

Users should be given a meaningful choice to accept or refuse optional categories and should be able to change or withdraw their choices through the website's cookie controls where such controls are implemented.

我们对 Cookie 的处理方式

Lantara Global 采用注重隐私保护的网站技术处理方式。

我们的目标是仅使用网站运行、安全以及用户所要求功能合理必要的技术，同时使用能够为合法业务或用户需求提供合理价值的可选技术。

如果适用法律要求在使用非必要 Cookie 或类似技术前取得同意，我们将在启用该技术前取得用户同意。分析及广告/跟踪技术不应仅因为对 Lantara Global 有用，就被视为严格必要。

在适用情况下，用户应能够对可选类别作出真实选择，并可通过网站的 Cookie 控制工具更改或撤回其选择。

4. Categories of Cookies

Depending on the website configuration, Lantara Global may use: (1) Strictly necessary cookies for core functions, security, authentication and sessions; (2) Preference or functional cookies for language and interface choices; (3) Analytics or performance cookies to understand website use and improve the site; (4) Marketing, advertising or tracking cookies, if implemented; and (5) Third-party cookies or similar technologies set by integrated services. Optional categories will be controlled in accordance with applicable consent requirements.

Cookie 类别

根据网站的实际配置，Lantara Global 可能使用：(1) 用于核心功能、安全、身份验证及会话管理的严格必要 Cookie；(2) 用于语言及界面偏好的功能 Cookie；(3) 用于了解网站使用情况及改进网站的分析或性能 Cookie；(4) 如实施，则用于营销、广告或跟踪的 Cookie；以及 (5) 由集成服务设置的第三方 Cookie 或类似技术。可选类别将按照适用的同意要求进行控制。

5. Strictly Necessary Cookies

Strictly necessary cookies may be used where they are genuinely required to provide the online service or functionality requested by the user. Examples may include secure login sessions, authentication, account security, fraud prevention, essential session state, load balancing, technical operation and remembering cookie-consent choices. These cookies are not used for advertising or unrelated tracking purposes. Technologies that are merely convenient, useful for internal analytics or used for advertising will not be classified as strictly necessary solely on that basis.

严格必要 Cookie

如果确实需要某些 Cookie 才能向用户提供其所要求的在线服务或功能，则可以使用严格必要 Cookie。例如，可用于安全登录会话、身份验证、账户安全、防止欺诈、维持必要会话状态、负载均衡、技术运行以及记住 Cookie 同意选择。这些 Cookie 不用于广告或无关跟踪。仅仅因为某项技术方便、对内部分析有用或用于广告，并不会使其自动成为严格必要技术。

6. Analytics and Performance Technologies

If Lantara Global implements analytics or performance technologies, they may be used to understand page visits, traffic sources, device or browser information, general usage patterns and website performance. Analytics technologies are not assumed to be strictly necessary merely because they help us improve the website. Where consent is legally required, analytics will be disabled until the user provides the relevant consent. Lantara Global will seek to configure analytics tools in a privacy-conscious manner and limit information collected to what is reasonably necessary for the stated purpose.

分析及性能技术

如果 Lantara Global 实施分析或性能技术，这些技术可能用于了解页面访问量、流量来源、设备或浏览器信息、一般使用模式以及网站性能。分析技术不会仅因为有助于我们改进网站，就被视为严格必要技术。在法律要求获得同意的情况下，分析技术将在用户提供相关同意之前保持禁用。Lantara Global 将以注重隐私保护的方式配置分析工具，并将收集的信息限制在实现所述目的合理必要的范围内。

7. Functional and Preference Technologies

Lantara Global may use technologies that remember choices made by a user, such as language selection, display preferences or other settings. Some preference technologies may fall within an applicable exemption where they are used solely to provide functionality or appearance requested by the user. Other persistent or multi-purpose technologies may require consent. Where consent is required, the technology will be treated as optional.

功能及偏好技术

Lantara Global 可能使用用于记住用户选择的技术，例如语言选择、显示偏好或其他设置。某些偏好技术如果仅用于提供用户所要求的功能或显示效果，可能符合适用的豁免条件。其他持久性或具有多重用途的技术可能需要获得同意。在需要获得同意的情况下，相关技术将被视为可选技术。

8. Marketing, Advertising and Tracking Technologies

Lantara Global does not need advertising cookies to provide the core recruitment service. If marketing, advertising, retargeting, social-media pixels or similar tracking technologies are introduced, they will be treated as optional and activated only in accordance with applicable consent requirements. Users should be able to refuse these technologies without losing access to core website content or recruitment services. Third-party providers may process information according to their own policies.

营销、广告及跟踪技术

Lantara Global 不需要使用广告 Cookie 来提供核心招聘服务。如果未来引入营销、广告、再营销、社交媒体像素或类似跟踪技术，这些技术将被视为可选技术，并仅在符合适用同意要求的情况下启用。用户应能够拒绝这些技术，而不会因此失去对核心网站内容或招聘服务的访问权限。第三方服务提供商可能根据其自身政策处理信息。

9. Authentication and Account Technologies

Where the website provides candidate or employer accounts, authentication and account-management technologies may be used to maintain secure sessions, protect accounts and provide requested account functionality. These technologies may be essential to the account service. Their precise operation will depend on the authentication and hosting infrastructure actually implemented by Lantara Global.

身份验证及账户技术

如果 Lantara Global 网站提供候选人或雇主账户，则可能使用身份验证及账户管理技术来维持安全会话、保护账户并提供用户要求的账户功能。这些技术可能是账户服务正常运行所必需的。其具体运行方式将取决于 Lantara Global 实际采用的身份验证及托管基础设施。

10. Third-Party Services

Some website functions may depend on third-party services, potentially including authentication, hosting and security, analytics, embedded media, maps, social-media services, recruitment tools or other integrations. A third party may set or access cookies or similar technologies when its service is used. Lantara Global will seek to identify relevant third parties and their purposes in the live cookie inventory and consent mechanism. Lantara Global will not intentionally activate optional third-party tracking technologies before obtaining consent required by applicable law.

第三方服务

某些网站功能可能依赖第三方服务，例如身份验证、托管及安全、分析、嵌入式媒体、地图、社交媒体、招聘工具或其他集成服务。使用第三方服务时，第三方可能设置或访问 Cookie 或类似技术。Lantara Global 将在实际 Cookie 清单及同意机制中尽可能明确相关第三方及其用途。Lantara Global 不会在未取得适用法律要求的同意前，故意启用可选的第三方跟踪技术。

11. Cookie Consent and Your Choices

Where applicable law requires consent for non-essential cookies or similar technologies, Lantara Global will provide a mechanism allowing users to make a clear and informed choice. The mechanism should allow users to accept or reject optional cookies, select categories where appropriate, review purposes, and change or withdraw preferences where supported. Continued browsing alone will not be treated as consent where affirmative consent is required. A strictly necessary technology may be used to remember the user's cookie-consent choice.

Cookie 同意及您的选择

在适用法律要求对非必要 Cookie 或类似技术取得同意的情况下，Lantara Global 将提供允许用户作出明确知情选择的机制。该机制应允许用户接受或拒绝可选 Cookie、在适当情况下选择类别、查看用途，并在网站支持的情况下更改或撤回偏好。在法律要求积极同意的情况下，Lantara Global 不会仅根据用户继续浏览网站而视为同意。为了记住用户的 Cookie 同意选择，网站可能需要使用严格必要技术。

12. Managing Cookies Through Your Browser

Most modern browsers allow users to delete, block or restrict cookies through browser settings. Users can generally block all cookies, block third-party cookies, delete existing cookies or clear stored data when closing the browser. Disabling strictly necessary cookies may prevent some website features, account functions or security controls from working correctly. Where the website provides a cookie-preference tool, users should use it to manage optional categories.

通过浏览器管理 Cookie

大多数现代浏览器允许用户通过浏览器设置删除、阻止或限制 Cookie。用户通常可以选择阻止所有 Cookie、阻止第三方 Cookie、删除现有 Cookie，或在关闭浏览器时清除存储的数据。禁用严格必要 Cookie 可能导致部分网站功能、账户功能或安全控制无法正常工作。如果网站提供 Cookie 偏好设置工具，用户应使用该工具管理可选类别。

13. Cookie Retention and Review

Cookies may be session-based or persistent. Lantara Global will seek to ensure that cookie duration is appropriate for the purpose for which the technology is used. Cookie durations will be reviewed when the website or relevant third-party services materially change. The live cookie inventory should identify, where technically available, the name, provider, first- or third-party status, purpose, category, duration and whether consent is required.

Cookie 保存期限及审查

Cookie 可以是会话 Cookie，也可以是持久性 Cookie。Lantara Global 将确保 Cookie 的保存期限与其使用目的相适应。当网站或相关第三方服务发生重大变化时，将审查 Cookie 的保存期限。实际 Cookie 清单应在技术可行的情况下注明名称、提供商、第一方或第三方属性、用途、类别、保存期限以及是否需要取得同意。

14. Relationship With the Privacy Policy

Cookies and similar technologies may involve processing personal information, depending on how they are configured and what information they collect. Where cookie-related processing involves personal information, the Lantara Global Website Privacy Policy and applicable data-protection requirements also apply. The Cookie Policy explains storage and access technologies; the Privacy Policy provides broader information about personal-data collection, use, disclosure, retention, security and user rights.

与隐私政策的关系

根据 Cookie 及类似技术的配置和收集的信息，这些技术可能涉及个人信息处理。如果与 Cookie 相关的处理涉及个人信息，Lantara Global《网站隐私政策》及适用的数据保护要求也将适用。Cookie 政策说明网站使用的存储及访问技术；隐私政策则更广泛地说明个人信息的收集、使用、披露、保存、安全措施及用户权利。

15. Changes to This Cookie Policy

Lantara Global may update this Cookie Policy when website technology, services, legal requirements or cookie practices change. The updated version will identify its version number and effective or updated date. Where material changes affect user choices or consent, Lantara Global will provide appropriate notice or request fresh consent where required. The current version will be made available on the Lantara Global website.

本 Cookie 政策的变更

当网站技术、服务、法律要求或 Cookie 使用方式发生变化时，Lantara Global 可以更新本 Cookie 政策。更新版本将注明版本号以及生效日期或更新日期。如果重大变更影响用户选择或同意，Lantara Global 将在需要时提供适当通知或重新取得同意。当前版本将在 Lantara Global 网站上提供。
