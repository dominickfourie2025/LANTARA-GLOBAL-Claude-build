Requirements vary depending on the employer, but most schools typically require:
  • Native English speaker from an eligible country
  • Bachelor's degree
  • Recognised TEFL/TESOL qualification (where applicable)
  • Relevant teaching experience (depending on the role)
  • Clean criminal background check
