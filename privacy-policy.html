Privacy Policy

1. Who We Are

Lantara Global is a trading name of Justin Dominick Fourie. Lantara Global operates as an international recruitment business focused on connecting qualified teachers with education employers in China. For this Policy, “Lantara Global”, “we”, “us” and “our” refer to Lantara Global and, where applicable, Justin Dominick Fourie in his capacity as the person operating the business under that trading name. This Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, retain and protect personal information through our website, candidate services, employer services and recruitment activities.

我们是谁

Lantara Global 是 Justin Dominick Fourie 使用的商业名称。Lantara Global 是一家国际招聘业务，主要负责将符合条件的教师与中国的教育机构雇主进行对接。在本政策中，“Lantara Global”、“我们”、“我方”及“我们的”均指 Lantara Global；在适用情况下，也指以该商业名称经营业务的 Justin Dominick Fourie。本政策说明我们如何通过网站、候选人服务、雇主服务及招聘活动收集、使用、披露、保存和保护个人信息。

2. Information We Collect

We collect only information reasonably necessary for recruitment, candidate management, employer services, legal and compliance requirements, security and legitimate business administration. This may include: website technical information and cookies; candidate identity, contact, nationality, residence, professional, education, employment, CV, teaching, availability, recruitment and work-authorisation information; candidate documents such as passports, qualifications, Apostilles, police clearances where required, visas and work permits; employer contact, company, recruitment, job, contractual and billing information; and information reasonably obtained from employers, references, recruitment platforms, service providers and competent authorities where legally permitted. We may process sensitive personal information where reasonably necessary for a legitimate recruitment, employment, immigration, compliance or operational purpose and where permitted by applicable law.

我们收集的信息

我们仅收集为招聘、候选人管理、雇主服务、法律及合规要求、安全以及合法业务管理而合理必要的信息。这可能包括：网站技术信息及 Cookie；候选人的身份、联系方式、国籍、居住地、职业、教育、工作经历、简历、教学经验、可工作时间、招聘及工作许可信息；护照、资格证书、Apostille（海牙认证）、在需要时提供的无犯罪记录证明、签证和工作许可等候选人文件；雇主的联系人、公司、招聘、职位、合同及账单信息；以及在法律允许范围内从雇主、推荐人、招聘平台、服务商和有管辖权的机构合理取得的信息。在合法招聘、就业、移民、合规或运营目的确有合理必要且适用法律允许的情况下，我们可能处理敏感个人信息。

3. Why We Use Personal Information

We may use personal information for recruitment and candidate placement; employer services and hiring requirements; legitimate employment, visa and work-authorisation processes; communications; identity, qualification and document verification and fraud prevention; legal and regulatory compliance; security; records, account administration, disputes and enforcement of agreements; retaining relevant candidate profiles for future opportunities where legally permitted; and website analytics and improvement. We will not use personal information for purposes incompatible with those described in this Policy unless permitted or required by law or the individual has provided any consent required by law.

我们为何使用个人信息

我们可能将个人信息用于招聘及候选人安置；雇主服务及了解招聘需求；合法的就业、签证及工作许可办理；沟通；身份、资格和文件核实及防止欺诈；法律和监管合规；安全；记录保存、账户管理、争议解决及协议执行；在法律允许情况下保留候选人资料以提供未来机会；以及网站分析和改进。除非适用法律允许或要求，或者个人已提供法律所要求的同意，我们不会将个人信息用于与本政策所述目的不相容的用途。

4. Who We May Share Information With

We may share personal information where reasonably necessary for the purposes in this Policy or where permitted or required by law. Recipients may include education employers; service providers supporting hosting, cloud storage, databases, email, recruitment systems, communications, payments, cybersecurity, document processing or analytics; immigration, labour, visa and other competent authorities; professional advisers; parties where disclosure is necessary to comply with law, protect rights or safety, investigate fraud, enforce agreements or establish or defend legal claims; and a successor entity in a legitimate sale, merger, restructuring or business transfer. We do not sell Candidate personal information to third parties.

我们可能向哪些对象共享信息

我们仅在为实现本政策所述目的而合理必要，或法律允许或要求的情况下共享个人信息。接收方可能包括教育机构雇主；为托管、云存储、数据库、电子邮件、招聘系统、通信、支付、网络安全、文件处理或分析提供支持的服务商；移民、劳动、签证及其他有管辖权的机构；专业顾问；为遵守法律、保护权利或安全、调查欺诈、执行协议或提出/抗辩法律请求而必要的相关方；以及在合法出售、合并、重组或业务转让情况下的承接实体。我们不会向第三方出售候选人的个人信息。

5. International and Cross-Border Transfers

Because Lantara Global provides international recruitment services, personal information may be processed in or transferred to countries other than the country where an individual resides. Where a candidate is considered for a position with a Chinese employer, relevant information may be transferred to China for recruitment, employment, visa or work-authorisation purposes. Some technology or service providers may also process information outside the candidate’s country of residence. We will take reasonable steps to ensure international transfers and cross-border access comply with applicable data-protection laws and required safeguards, mechanisms, notifications or consents. Where Chinese law applies to a cross-border transfer, we will comply with applicable requirements.

国际及跨境数据传输

由于 Lantara Global 提供国际招聘服务，个人信息可能在个人居住国以外的国家或地区被处理或传输。当候选人申请中国雇主的职位时，相关信息可能因招聘、就业、签证或工作许可办理而传输至中国。部分技术或服务提供商也可能在候选人居住国以外处理信息。我们将采取合理措施，确保国际传输和跨境访问符合适用的数据保护法律以及所要求的保护措施、机制、通知或同意要求。如果个人信息的跨境传输适用中国法律，我们将遵守相关要求。

6. Retention and Deletion

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment, future recruitment opportunities, legal and contractual obligations, dispute resolution, security and legitimate business administration. Active candidate information may be retained while an application, interview, placement, employment or visa/work-authorisation process is ongoing. Relevant information may be retained after placement for placement administration, contractual or legal records, employer relationship management and potential future recruitment. Where legally permitted, candidate profiles may remain in the Talent Pool for as long as reasonably necessary. Candidates may request deletion of their Lantara Global profile and personal information at any time. We will deactivate the profile, remove the candidate from active recruitment and future Talent Pool consideration, and delete or anonymise information where reasonably practicable and legally permitted. Limited information may be retained where legally required or reasonably necessary for legal claims, fraud prevention, essential records or another lawful purpose. Deletion does not affect the lawfulness of processing before the request.

信息保存期限及删除

我们仅在为招聘、未来招聘机会、法律和合同义务、争议解决、安全及合法业务管理而合理必要的期间内保存个人信息。活跃候选人的信息可在申请、面试、安置、就业或签证/工作许可办理期间保存。安置后，相关信息可用于安置管理、合同或法律记录、雇主关系管理及未来招聘。法律允许时，候选人资料可在合理必要期间保留于人才库。候选人可随时要求删除其 Lantara Global 个人资料及个人信息。我们将停用资料，将其从当前招聘及未来人才库考虑范围中移除，并在合理可行且法律允许的情况下删除或匿名化信息。在法律要求或为法律请求、防止欺诈、保存必要记录或其他合法目的而合理必要的情况下，我们可能保留有限信息。删除请求不影响此前处理行为的合法性。

7. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to applicable law, individuals may have rights to receive information about processing; access personal information; correct inaccurate or incomplete information; request deletion; withdraw consent where processing is based on consent; opt out of future recruitment contact; request information concerning relevant cross-border processing; make a privacy complaint; and request closure of a Lantara Global candidate account. Requests may be sent to hello@lantaraglobal.com. We may reasonably verify identity where necessary. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law. Rights are not absolute and requests may be declined or limited where permitted or required by law, with reasons provided where legally permitted.

您的隐私权利

在适用法律范围内，个人可能有权获知处理情况；访问个人信息；更正不准确或不完整的信息；要求删除；在处理依据为同意时撤回同意；选择不再接收未来招聘联系；了解相关跨境处理；提出隐私投诉；以及要求关闭 Lantara Global 候选人账户。请求可发送至 hello@lantaraglobal.com。必要时我们可能采取合理措施核实身份。我们将在适用法律规定的期限内回复。上述权利并非绝对权利；在法律允许或要求的情况下，我们可能拒绝或限制请求，并在法律允许的情况下说明原因。

8. Cookies, Analytics and Website Tracking

Lantara Global may use cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure and improve its website, remember essential preferences, maintain sessions, analyse website usage and understand visitor interactions. Where required by applicable law, we will obtain consent before using non-essential cookies or similar tracking technologies. Visitors may control or disable cookies through browser settings, although this may affect functionality. Where analytics technologies are used, they will be used for legitimate purposes such as understanding traffic, diagnosing technical issues and improving the website and services. The Lantara Global MVP does not intend to use advertising or behavioural tracking technologies to sell visitor information or build advertising profiles.

Cookie、分析及网站追踪

Lantara Global 可能使用 Cookie 及类似技术，以运营、保护和改进网站，记住必要的偏好设置、维持会话、分析网站使用情况以及了解访客互动。在适用法律要求的情况下，我们将在使用非必要 Cookie 或类似追踪技术前取得必要同意。访客可通过浏览器设置控制或禁用 Cookie，但这可能影响网站功能。如使用分析技术，其用途将限于了解流量、诊断技术问题以及改进网站和服务。Lantara Global 的 MVP 版本无意使用广告或行为追踪技术来出售访客信息或建立广告画像。

9. Security and Data Protection

Lantara Global will take reasonable and appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, loss, misuse, alteration, destruction and accidental disclosure. Depending on the systems and services involved, safeguards may include secure authentication, access controls, encryption where appropriate, secure transmission, secure storage, backups, monitoring and other security controls. Access will be limited according to legitimate need. If we become aware of a security incident involving personal information, we will investigate it, take reasonable containment and mitigation measures, and comply with applicable notification obligations. We will notify affected individuals where required or appropriate. Lantara Global does not intentionally request passwords, banking passwords, authentication codes or other credentials unrelated to legitimate recruitment services. Candidates should keep credentials confidential and notify us of suspected unauthorised access.

安全及数据保护

Lantara Global 将采取合理且适当的技术和组织措施，保护个人信息免受未经授权的访问、披露、丢失、滥用、修改、毁坏及意外泄露。根据所使用的系统和服务，保护措施可能包括安全身份验证、访问控制、在适当情况下加密、安全传输、安全存储、备份、监控及其他安全控制措施。访问权限将根据合法业务需要进行限制。如果我们知悉涉及个人信息的安全事件，我们将进行调查，采取合理的控制和减轻影响措施，并遵守适用的通知义务。在法律要求或适当的情况下，我们将通知受影响的个人。Lantara Global 不会故意要求候选人提供与合法招聘服务无关的密码、银行密码、身份验证代码或其他账户凭证。候选人应妥善保管凭证，并在怀疑存在未经授权的访问时通知我们。

10. Children and Age Requirements

Lantara Global’s recruitment services are intended for adults who are legally able to enter employment and recruitment arrangements. Candidates must generally be at least 18 years old unless applicable law expressly permits otherwise. Candidate registration may include an age declaration. If we become aware that we have collected personal information from a person under 18 without an appropriate legal basis, we will take reasonable steps to delete it, subject to applicable legal requirements. Our services are not directed at children and we do not intentionally seek to recruit minors.

儿童及年龄要求

Lantara Global 的招聘服务面向依法有能力签订就业及招聘相关安排的成年人。除非适用法律明确允许其他情况，候选人通常必须年满 18 周岁。候选人注册时可能需要进行年龄声明。如果我们发现自己在没有适当法律依据的情况下收集了未满 18 周岁人员的个人信息，我们将根据适用法律要求采取合理措施删除相关信息。我们的服务并非面向儿童，我们不会主动招募未成年人。
