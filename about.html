About Lantara Global

Building Long-Term Partnerships Through Education

We connect Chinese schools with carefully screened, native English-speaking teachers and help international educators build meaningful careers in China. Our mission is to create value for schools, teachers and students through trust, expertise and long-term commitment.

Founder's Story

Experience. Perspective. Purpose.

With 15 years of experience in Asia's education industry, our founder understands the classroom and the recruitment process from the inside — years of teaching, educational leadership and recruitment across Asia, giving Lantara Global a unique perspective on what schools truly need and what teachers are looking for.

Lantara Global was founded to bring a higher standard of professionalism, transparency and long-term partnership to international teacher recruitment in China.
